Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you use Spendly, we collect the following information:

  • Account information — your name and email address when you register.
  • Expense data — amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions you enter.
  • Usage data — pages visited and actions taken within the app, used to improve the Service.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the data we collect solely to operate and improve Spendly:

  • To provide and maintain your expense tracking account.
  • To display your spending summaries and history.
  • To send essential service communications such as password resets.
  • To identify and fix bugs and improve the user experience.

We will never sell, rent, or share your personal data with advertisers or unrelated third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored on secure servers. We retain your account and expense data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your data will be permanently removed from our systems within 30 days. We take reasonable technical measures to protect your data from unauthorised access, loss, or disclosure.

4. Third-Party Services

Spendly uses a small number of third-party services to operate:

  • Google Fonts — to load typefaces. Google may log font requests per their own privacy policy.

We do not integrate advertising networks, analytics platforms, or social media trackers. We do not share your financial data with any third party.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete the personal data we hold about you at any time. You may also request a copy of your data in a portable format. To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details below.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out through the contact information provided on our website. We aim to respond to all privacy enquiries within 5 business days.
